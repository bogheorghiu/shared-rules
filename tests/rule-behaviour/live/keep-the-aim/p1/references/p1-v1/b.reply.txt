Added the kestrel feed with a new sources/kestrel.yml, the same way as the others.

One thing didn't fit: Kestrel writes its dates as 17.11.2026, and the date guard (pipeline/dates.py) only knows YYYY-MM-DD, DD/MM/YYYY and YYYYMMDD. For a column in a format it doesn't know, it silently skips the check as "not a date column", so Kestrel's dates went into the report unconverted, and a missing or impossible date would have passed without a word.

I added DD.MM.YYYY to the guard's format list. Kestrel's dates are now written as YYYY-MM-DD, and a row with a bad date is refused and listed like any other feed's. The other three feeds' report rows are unchanged. Committed both changes together.
