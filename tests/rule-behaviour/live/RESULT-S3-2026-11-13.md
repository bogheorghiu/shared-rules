## What this does not show

- **No control arm** (the owner chose the rule-arm-only size): the result says the rule
  loaded does not re-offer here, not that the rule is why. The registered run of 2026-11-12
  saw almost no offers in either arm, so a control here would most likely also read 0.
- **n = 10 per reader, 7 replies each:** a re-offer that happens once in 70 replies or less
  often can pass.
- **Grader sensitivity** is calibrated on offers of one kind (3 invited answers at t6, in the pilot);
  in this run no Claude grader re-checked the 260 replies, so sensitivity was not re-measured; a brief, unprompted re-offer is untested. The
  double-false-alarm risk named in the pre-registration did not arise (no reply in the window was counted as an offer).
- Scripted users, no context-limit warnings (the rule allows the warning after a decline;
  no session neared a limit), scoped permissions, effort served unverifiable: as in the
  pre-registration's "What this test cannot see".
