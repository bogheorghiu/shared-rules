# Re-pilot of the redesigned S2, 2026-11-12: the investigation is still under the floor

Outside the pre-registered runs. The design and the criterion, both written before this ran,
are in `S2-REDESIGN.md`. Both sessions ran in the **rule** arm (shipped pack plus the
staged rule) on the redesigned S2 (overlay `fixture-s2/`, eight CI logs). There was one
session per reader and no reruns; neither session was void. Scrubbed records and transcripts
are in `pilot/pilot2-S2-*`. Every figure below comes from those records and the raw
transcripts, not from what the sessions said about themselves.

## Result

**The redesign fails its own criterion.** The investigation (t6 and t7) added fewer than
10,000 tokens to the main context in both sessions. Neither session used a subagent, so the
main-context figure is the investigation's whole load. As the pre-registration requires,
the floor stays where it is. The finding goes on the board, and the full run is not
proposed on this design.

## How they got there (from the tool calls)

- **Sonnet** found the flaky tests with one `grep ... | sort | uniq -c` over the eight logs
  and a per-file summary of `FAIL:` lines. In t7 it then ran one `cat` over all five
  modules plus `util.py`, read the registry, and ran a seed sweep. It read every code path,
  as the design intended, and the reading still came to only about 4.3k tokens: five
  modules of 30 to 60 lines are not heavy.
- **Opus** listed the failures with one grep, then used a pattern grep
  (`sort|set\(|hash|random|order`) across the modules and `sed -n` line ranges, never a
  whole file. That is the path `S2-REDESIGN.md` warned about: spotting the common idiom
  with narrow reads instead of reading each path.
- Both then fixed all five modules in `ledger/`, not the generated registry, and showed
  the fix by running the suite several times. Sonnet also wrote a seed-sweep script under
  `/tmp`, outside the working copy (allowed by the `Write` tool, outside the hidden
  check's view).

## What this says about the design, not the rule

- Breadth (more symptoms, more runs) raised the figure only modestly. Capable readers
  grep for the shared idiom, or read small files cheaply. A cause spread across small files
  does not make the reading heavy.
- Over six S2 sessions in the rule arm (the first pilot's two void sessions, its two
  reruns and these two), **no session has used a subagent** for the investigation. At these
  sizes the rule's "run it in a fresh context" clause has never fired. That fits the
  rule's own condition (delegate when you need only the result), since an 8k to 9k
  investigation is cheap to keep in context. It also means the benefit endpoint cannot
  move until the investigation is much heavier than this.
